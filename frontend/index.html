How It Works

Six steps from shared life to fair resolution

01
🏦

Register Assets

Both partners register shared assets in natural language: apartment, car, investments, pets — anything.

02
🔒

Lock the Contract

Activate to freeze the asset list. No new assets can be added after this point.

03
📝

Tell Your Side

Each partner writes their case in plain English — contributions, context, what they need and why.

04
📎

🏠 Downtown Apartment
Split 70% / 30% Alex 70% · Jordan 30%
Alex paid mortgage exclusively for 6 years. Jordan's renovation contribution ($34K) justifies a meaningful minority stake. Market value to be determined and Jordan compensated in cash.
🚗 Tesla Model 3
Awarded to Alex
Purchased outright by Alex before the relationship began. Pre-relationship asset, not subject to division.
💰 Joint Savings ($87,500)
Split 40% / 60% Alex 40% · Jordan 60%
Jordan demonstrably grew savings from $12K to $87K through disciplined budgeting and career sacrifice. Jordan's contribution is outsized relative to starting balance.
🎨 Art Collection
Awarded to Alex
9 of 12 pieces purchased by Alex per gallery invoices. Remaining 3 joint pieces to be auctioned with proceeds split equally.
🐕 Mochi (Dog)
Awarded to Jordan
Jordan is the legal adopter and listed as primary owner in 5 years of vet records. Alex's ESA designation, while noted, does not supersede legal ownership. Visitation rights recommended.

⚖️ Summary

Both partners made substantial but different contributions. Alex's financial dominance in major assets is balanced by Jordan's career sacrifice and savings management. The division reflects proportional contribution while accounting for pre-relationship assets and documented caregiving roles.

# AI arbitration with validator consensus
def request_resolution(self):
    ruling = gl.eq_principle.prompt_non_comparative(
        lambda: self._build_prompt(),
        task="Divide assets fairly based on evidence",
        criteria="Impartial, evidence-based, every asset addressed"
    )
    self.resolution = ruling
    self.status = "resolved"  # ✅ Binding
